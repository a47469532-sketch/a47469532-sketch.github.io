Tongyeok Privacy Policy

Effective: November 17, 2026

Tongyeok does not collect any personal data.

  1. Audio and text — Microphone and system audio are transcribed and translated only on your Mac, using Apple's on-device speech recognition and translation. Nothing is sent to any server.
  2. Meeting records and glossary — Stored only on your Mac, in the app's own container. You can delete them anytime in the app; they are removed when you delete the app.
  3. Email and PDF — An email draft or a PDF in your Downloads folder is created only when you choose to. You decide whether to send it.
  4. Permissions — Microphone and system audio recording are used only to create subtitles. No audio files are saved.
  5. Analytics, ads, tracking — None. No account or sign-up.
  6. Contact — ratm0001@naver.com
